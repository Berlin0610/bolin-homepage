About

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from City University of Hong Kong in February 2025. In March 2025, I joined Alibaba DAMO Academy as a Senior Algorithm Engineer and started a Joint Postdoctoral Fellow at Fudan University.

My Ph.D. research focused on video coding and standardization, and since then my work has expanded to frontier 3D vision and its practical deployment.

Research Interests

  • 3D Vision Research and Productization. 3D reconstruction, generation, and deployment.
  • Generative Visual Compression. Compression for image, video, and visual content with generative methods.
  • Ultra-Low-Bitrate Avatar Coding. Efficient representation of talking faces and human avatars.
  • Perception-aware Visual Computing. Perceptual quality assessment, human-centric image/video processing, and generative media detection.
